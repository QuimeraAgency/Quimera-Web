§ 01 · CONTEXT
The Opportunity

Spain as Hiya's European reputation testbed.

A market where regulation, consumer behaviour and business need are converging around the same issue — the loss of trust in the voice channel.

Consumers are increasingly reluctant to answer unknown calls. Businesses face growing challenges in reaching customers. Fraud-related concerns continue to shape public conversation.

The voice channel is no longer just a communications tool. It is a trust issue.

For Hiya, Spain can become a strategic proof point — a market where proprietary data, local media visibility, telecom relationships and stakeholder engagement work together to establish the company as a credible authority on trusted calls, spam prevention and phone fraud.

By building position in Spain first, Hiya can create a communications model that is locally relevant, commercially useful and scalable across other priority European markets.

§ 02 · APPROACH
The Approach

Five moves, not five services.

From market presence to market relevance — a sequence designed to compound.
01

Define the local narrative

Translate Hiya's global positioning into a Spanish market story — articulating why Hiya matters in Spain, what problem it helps solve, and how its technology and data contribute to rebuilding trust.

02

Turn data into authority

Use Hiya's proprietary insights to create media angles, thought-leadership opportunities and recurring conversations around spam, fraud and consumer behaviour.

03

Build the right visibility

Targeted exposure in the media, forums and industry conversations that build credibility with telecom players, business decision-makers and ecosystem stakeholders.

04

Position leadership

Establish Hiya's spokespeople — primarily Mariano González-Cayuela — as credible expert voices through messaging, interview prep, opinion angles and speaking opportunities.

05

Scale to Europe

Use Spain as Hiya's first local reputation hub — testing messaging, refining content, activating data-led narratives. Develop a playbook adaptable to France and Germany.
