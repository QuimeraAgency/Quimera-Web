Regulatory Narrative
Spain is not simply a new market for Hiya. It is one of the first European markets where
regulation, consumer behavior and business need are converging around the same issue: the
loss of trust in the voice channel.
The introduction of the 400 prefix and Ley 10/2025 is reshaping how companies can contact
consumers, at a time when a large part of the population already avoids answering unknown
calls due to spam and fraud concerns. The result is a growing challenge for legitimate
businesses that still depend on phone communications to operate.
This creates a clear opportunity for Hiya: not only to help identify calls, but to help rebuild trust
in who is calling and why the call deserves to be answered.
Recommended narrative frame
From nuisance calls to trusted voice infrastructure
The conversation in Spain should move beyond
“how do we reduce unwanted calls?” towards a
broader and more strategic question: how do we
make the voice channel trustworthy again?
For Hiya, this is the opportunity to move from being
perceived as an anti-spam solution to being
positioned as part of the trust infrastructure that
enables consumers to answer calls with more
confidence and legitimate businesses to protect
one of their most important customer contact
channels.
Yes, we will need to also help educate the
market as we shift the conversation.
